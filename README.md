A personal continuity engine for high-agency people.

## Thesis
Most productivity tools help people capture tasks, notes, and plans, but they do very little to preserve continuity of intention across changing moods, energy, context, and time. For high-agency, idea-rich people, the real problem is not input, it is re-entry: knowing what still matters, what has drifted, and what the truest next move is after interruption or fragmentation.

This project explores a personal continuity engine that turns a person's taste, priorities, memory, and active projects into a durable operating surface, so they can recover momentum, delegate safely to agents, and live inside a system that reflects who they actually are rather than forcing them into generic productivity theatre.

# Identity Extraction Notes

## Core idea
The app should not begin by asking the user to organise tasks.
It should begin by helping them reveal something true about themselves.

A likely early truth is that many adults are still oriented around the symbolic world that first made them feel alive.
Not necessarily childhood in a literal sense, but an early emotional reality, mythology, or private cosmology that still shapes what they value, chase, avoid, and return to.

Examples:
- computers, America, progress, building
- sport, discipline, competition
- art, romance, melancholy
- mysticism, drugs, stars, altered consciousness
- fantasy, games, stories, escape
- status, money, power

The system should treat this not as a novelty insight but as foundational identity context.

## Product implication
The app’s first job is not productivity capture.
Its first job is identity extraction through willing truth-sharing.

It should help the user articulate things like:
- what early world first made life feel charged with meaning
- what symbols, moods, or environments they still orbit
- what kinds of reality they remain loyal to
- what they are secretly always trying to get back to, extend, or redeem

## Why this matters
Without this layer, the system risks becoming another generic task shell.
With it, the system can build continuity around who the person actually is rather than around abstract obligation.

## Onboarding implication
Early onboarding should include prompts that feel more like self-recognition than setup.

Possible prompt directions:
- What kind of world first made you feel alive?
- What did you love before you knew how to explain yourself?
- What atmosphere, dream, or mythology have you never really left?
- What part of reality do you keep returning to, even in adult form?
- What are you still trying to build your life around, even if indirectly?

## Data model implication
This likely deserves a first-class identity layer later, not just raw journal text.
Potential future entity ideas:
- IdentityTheme
- PersonalMythology
- FoundationalWorld
- MeaningAnchor

## Design principle
The system should earn truth by asking unusually good questions.
It should not interrogate the user.
It should invite recognition.

## Role in continuity
This layer helps the app:
- interpret priorities more humanely
- explain recurring project attraction
- identify deeper continuity across apparently unrelated work
- distinguish real desire from inherited obligation

## Working product statement
A personal continuity system should begin by asking:

**What private world are you still living inside?**

Not to trap the person there, but to understand the hidden continuity they are already carrying.

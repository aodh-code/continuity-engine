# Business Model

## Initial model
Start as a high-trust, narrow, premium service-product hybrid rather than mass-market SaaS.

## Core offer
A personal continuity system for high-agency operators.

## First customers
- solo founders
- writers/builders
- product leaders
- creative operators
- ADHD-ish high-functioning people
- people with complex self-directed lives

## Delivery
- bespoke setup
- ongoing continuity maintenance
- later, software productization of common patterns

## Role split
Aodh/OpenClaw can help run memory, summaries, workflow hygiene, and internal ops.
Brendan remains the human authority for trust, judgment, approvals, and external commitments.
